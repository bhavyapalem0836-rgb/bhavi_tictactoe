How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a simple two-player game played on a 3×3 grid.

1

Take Turns

Players take turns placing their mark (X goes first, followed by O) in any unoccupied space on the grid.

2

Make Three in a Row

The first player to place 3 of their marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row immediately wins the game.

3

Cat's Game (Draw)

If all 9 spaces are filled and neither player has achieved 3 in a row, the match ends in a draw.

Winning Tips & Strategies

Master the fundamental tactics to never lose a match.

🎯

Control the Center First

The center square is involved in 4 potential winning lines (1 horizontal, 1 vertical, and 2 diagonals). Controlling it gives you the strongest strategic foundation.

🛡️

Always Block Opponent Lines

Whenever your opponent gets two of their marks in any line, immediately place your mark in the third spot to block them from winning.

⚡

Create a Double Attack (Fork)

A fork occurs when you create two unblocked two-in-a-row threats simultaneously. Because the opponent can only block one, you are guaranteed a victory on your next move.
